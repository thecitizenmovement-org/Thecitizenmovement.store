ASSISTANT FILING SHEET — CJP JUDICIAL MISCONDUCT COMPLAINT
For the AI assistant to present to a paying customer alongside
explainer-sheet-cjp-complaint.txt. General information only, not
legal advice. Use the official CJP form — this sheet is not a form.

WHAT THIS IS FOR:
Asking California's Commission on Judicial Performance to
investigate a state judge's misconduct or incapacity, and, if
appropriate, discipline the judge. It does NOT change any ruling
in your case.

WHO FILES IT:
Any member of the public with a factual complaint about a judge
within the Commission's jurisdiction (superior court judges,
Court of Appeal and Supreme Court justices, and former judges for
pre-retirement conduct).

OFFICIAL ROUTE — DO THIS FIRST:
1. Go to https://cjp.ca.gov/file_a_complaint/ and use the Online
   Complaint Form, or print the Printable Complaint Form from
   that page.
2. If filing by mail/fax instead of online: Commission on Judicial
   Performance, 455 Golden Gate Avenue, Suite 14400, San Francisco,
   CA 94102; fax under 10 pages to (415) 557-1266.
3. If the officer is a commissioner or referee: file FIRST with
   the court where that officer serves; only then, if unsatisfied,
   file with the Commission within 30 days of the mailing of the
   court's response.

DEADLINES:
- No general filing deadline is stated on the Commission's filing
  page — file promptly while dates, witnesses, and records are
  fresh, and check the current CJP instructions before filing.
- The 30-day rule above applies ONLY to commissioner/referee
  complaints after a local-court response.

WHAT GOES IN IT — CHECKLIST:
[ ] Judge's full name and court
[ ] Case type, name, and number, and your relationship to the case
[ ] Date(s) of the conduct
[ ] Detailed factual description — what the judge did and said,
    in order, not conclusions ("rude" / "biased" alone will fail)
[ ] Witness names and telephone numbers
[ ] Copies (never originals) of documents, orders, transcripts,
    or recordings showing the misconduct, or a mention of them

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER FILING:
- The Commission reviews the complaint, may investigate, and may
  contact you for more information. The process can take a while.
- Possible outcomes are discipline-related only. No ruling in
  your case is changed by this complaint.

COMMON TRAPS:
- Filing a ruling disagreement as a misconduct complaint — it will
  not be investigated, and appeal is the route for rulings.
- Sending originals instead of copies.
- Trying to file by phone — the Commission does not accept
  complaints by phone.
- Skipping the local-court step for a commissioner/referee.
- Including legal analysis and canon arguments instead of facts.

PAIR WITH (SAME LIBRARY):
- Judge disqualification in the case itself: draft-judge-
  disqualification.txt (CCP 170.1 / 170.6).
- Challenging a ruling: draft-notice-of-appeal.txt.
