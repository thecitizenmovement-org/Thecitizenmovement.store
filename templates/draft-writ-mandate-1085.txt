VERIFIED PETITION FOR WRIT OF MANDATE
CALIFORNIA CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE SECTIONS 1085 AND 1086
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

INSTRUCTIONS: A writ of mandate compels a government body, officer,
or inferior tribunal to perform a mandatory, ministerial act the law
specially requires. It does NOT compel an official to exercise
discretion in a particular way. This template covers traditional
mandamus under CCP 1085. Administrative mandamus under CCP 1094.5
applies instead when you challenge a quasi-judicial agency decision
made after a formal hearing — use that procedure, with the
administrative record, for that kind of case. Check the superior
court's local rules, the civil case cover sheet, and any filing
deadline that applies to your matter (some writ deadlines are as
short as 90 days from the agency's final decision). Exhaust available
administrative remedies first unless an exception clearly applies.

STATUTE: CCP 1085(a) — a writ may issue to compel performance of an
act the law specially enjoins as a duty resulting from office, trust,
or station, or to compel admission to a right or office unlawfully
withheld. CCP 1086 — the writ must issue where there is no plain,
speedy, and adequate remedy in the ordinary course of law, upon the
verified petition of a party beneficially interested. CCP 1107 —
proof of service must accompany an application for a prerogative writ.

WHAT YOU MUST SHOW:
1. A CLEAR, PRESENT, MINISTERIAL DUTY on the respondent — the law
   says the respondent "shall" do the act, not merely that it "may."
2. A CLEAR, PRESENT, BENEFICIAL RIGHT in you to performance.
3. NO PLAIN, SPEEDY, AND ADEQUATE ALTERNATIVE REMEDY.
4. EXHAUSTION of administrative remedies, or a stated exception.

CAPTION — FILL IN:
[Your name], Petitioner, v. [Agency / officer name], Respondent;
[Real party in interest, if any]. Superior Court of California,
County of [___], Unlimited Civil Case. Petition for Writ of Mandate
(CCP 1085).

INTRODUCTION:
Petitioner seeks a writ of mandate compelling Respondent to [state
the exact act required, e.g., "issue the permit," "disclose the
records," "hold the hearing required by ___"].

STATEMENT OF FACTS — FILL IN, CHRONOLOGICALLY:
1. Petitioner is [identity and interest in the matter].
2. Respondent is [office/agency] with the duty described below.
3. On [dates], [what happened, what was requested, what was refused
   or not done]. Attach exhibits: requests, responses, statutes,
   agency decisions, correspondence.

LEGAL BASIS — FILL IN:
The duty: [Statute / regulation / ordinance] provides that
Respondent "shall" [act]. Quote the duty language verbatim.
No adequate remedy: [Explain why no other lawsuit, appeal, or
agency process gives the same relief, or why it is inadequate.]
Exhaustion: [List each administrative step taken and its outcome,
or state the exception relied on.]

PRAYER FOR RELIEF:
Petitioner prays that the court issue an alternative writ commanding
Respondent to [perform the act] or show cause why it has not done so,
and, after hearing, a peremptory writ commanding performance, plus
costs as allowed by law.

VERIFICATION:
I, [name], declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of
California that the foregoing is true and correct. Signed: [Name,
date, place].

SERVICE NOTE:
After filing, serve the petition and supporting papers on Respondent
and any real party in interest, and file proof of service (CCP 1107).
Check local rules for the hearing schedule, briefing deadlines, and
whether a proposed order to show cause is required.
