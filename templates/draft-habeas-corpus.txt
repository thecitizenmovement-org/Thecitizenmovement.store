PETITION FOR WRIT OF HABEAS CORPUS
CALIFORNIA PENAL CODE SECTION 1473; FEDERAL 28 U.S.C. SECTION 2254
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

INSTRUCTIONS: Habeas corpus challenges the fact or duration of
confinement — it is not a civil-rights damages suit and not a
substitute for a direct appeal. You must generally be in custody
(including probation, parole, or bail/recognizance in some
circumstances), you must generally have exhausted other remedies,
and the grounds must be new — not already decided against you on
appeal. Federal habeas under 28 U.S.C. 2254 has strict rules: you
must exhaust state remedies (present each claim to the California
Supreme Court by state habeas first), and a federal petition is
generally subject to a one-year limitations period. Federal courts
use their own habeas form — use the district court's form where
required, and file state habeas in the superior court, then the
Court of Appeal, then the Supreme Court, in that order, unless
counsel advises otherwise. This is a last-resort, high-stakes
petition — consult a licensed attorney if at all possible.

CALIFORNIA GROUNDS — PENAL CODE 1473(b) INCLUDES, BUT IS NOT
LIMITED TO:
(1) False evidence substantially material or probative on guilt or
    punishment was introduced at a hearing or trial relating to
    incarceration;
(2) Newly discovered evidence, and other grounds stated in the
    statute, including ineffective assistance of counsel and other
    constitutional violations — check the current text of Section
    1473 before filing and plead each ground separately with facts.

CAPTION — FILL IN:
In re [Petitioner full name], on Habeas Corpus. [State court: Superior
Court of California, County of ___; Federal court: United States
District Court for the ___ District of California.] Custodian:
[Warden / sheriff / agency, if known]. Case / conviction: [Court,
case number, date of judgment, sentence].

PETITION — FILL IN:
Petitioner is in custody at [facility / status] under [judgment].
Petitioner seeks relief on the following grounds, each stated
separately with supporting facts:
Ground 1: [Legal ground, e.g., "False evidence — Penal Code
1473(b)(1)"]. Facts: [Specific facts, dates, witnesses, exhibits.
State what evidence was false, why it was material, and how you
learned of it and when.]
[Repeat for each ground. Attach exhibits and a declaration.]
Exhaustion: [List each prior appeal, motion, and state habeas
petition filed, with court, case number, date, and outcome — or
explain why exhaustion does not apply to a ground.]
Relief requested: [Release, new trial, modified sentence, or other
relief the court may grant on the ground pleaded.]

VERIFICATION:
I, [name], declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is
true and correct, and that all grounds and supporting facts known
to me are included. Signed: [Name, date, place].

FEDERAL NOTE:
A federal petition under 28 U.S.C. 2254 must show exhaustion of
state remedies (28 U.S.C. 2254(b)) and compliance with the one-year
period in 28 U.S.C. 2244(d). Do not file federal habeas before
completing state habeas unless counsel advises that an exception
applies — an unexhausted federal petition may be dismissed.
