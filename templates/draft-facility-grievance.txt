CUSTODY FACILITY GRIEVANCE
(Jail / Prison Complaint)

INSTRUCTIONS: File through your facility's official grievance system
(ask any staff member or the law library for the grievance form and
procedure). ALWAYS keep your own copy with the grievance/tracking
number written on it. If the facility will not give you a form,
write your grievance on paper, date it, and hand it to staff --
then write down who you gave it to and when.

================================================================
READ THIS FIRST -- EXHAUSTION REQUIREMENT

Under federal law (the Prison Litigation Reform Act), you MUST
complete the facility's entire grievance/appeal process BEFORE you
can file a lawsuit about what happened. If you skip steps or miss
appeal deadlines, a court can throw out your case no matter how
strong it is. File every level of appeal, on time, and keep copies
of everything. This grievance is step one -- not the whole process.
================================================================

FILING NOTES: Submit through the facility's grievance system and get
a grievance/tracking number -- write it here: [NUMBER]. Keep YOUR
copy somewhere safe (with a trusted person outside if possible).
Calendar every appeal deadline the facility gives you and file each
appeal level even if the first response denies you -- that paper trail
IS your exhaustion of remedies. If the grievance involves a medical
emergency, also submit a sick-call slip the same day and keep a copy.
If staff refuse to give you forms or retaliate for filing, document
that in writing too -- retaliation for grievances is itself grievable
and actionable.
