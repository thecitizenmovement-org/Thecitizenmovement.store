MOTION TO SUPPRESS EVIDENCE / FOR RETURN OF PROPERTY
CALIFORNIA PENAL CODE SECTION 1538.5
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

INSTRUCTIONS: A Section 1538.5 motion asks the court to exclude
evidence, or return property, obtained by an unlawful search or
seizure. It must be in writing, with a memorandum of points and
authorities and proof of service, and it must list the specific
items sought to be suppressed and set forth the factual basis and
legal authorities for suppression. Timing matters: at a preliminary
examination, the motion must be filed and personally served at least
five court days before the examination, with limited continuance
rules if the grounds were not known earlier. Check the criminal
court's local rules and the case schedule. This motion addresses
Fourth Amendment search/seizure grounds; Fifth and Sixth Amendment
suppression issues are ordinarily raised by motion in limine, not
under Section 1538.5. Consult a licensed attorney.

STATUTE: Penal Code 1538.5(a)(1) — a defendant may move for return
of property or to suppress any tangible or intangible thing obtained
as a result of a search or seizure on these grounds:
(A) The search or seizure without a warrant was unreasonable.
(B) The search or seizure with a warrant was unreasonable because:
  (i) the warrant is insufficient on its face;
  (ii) the property/evidence obtained is not that described in the
      warrant;
  (iii) there was not probable cause for issuance of the warrant;
  (iv) the method of execution violated federal or state
      constitutional standards;
  (v) any other violation of federal or state constitutional
      standards.

CAPTION — FILL IN:

Notice of Motion and Motion to Suppress Evidence (Pen. Code 1538.5).

STATEMENT OF FACTS — FILL IN:
On [date], at [place], [who searched/seized what, with or without
a warrant, and the sequence of events]. State whether a warrant
existed, what it described, what was actually taken, and any facts
showing lack of probable cause, a facial defect, or an unlawful
method of execution. Attach or list: warrant and affidavit (if any),
inventory/receipt, reports, video, and witness names.

MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES — FILL IN:
1. Standing: Defendant had a reasonable expectation of privacy in
   [place / item / vehicle / home] because [facts].
2. The search/seizure was unlawful because [apply the chosen ground
   to the facts, with citations to the Fourth Amendment, Article I,
   section 13 of the California Constitution, and Penal Code 1538.5].
3. The evidence derived from the unlawful search, including [items],
   must also be suppressed as fruit of the unlawful search, unless
   the People establish an exception (list any exception you
   anticipate and why it does not apply).

PRAYER:
Defendant prays that the court suppress the items listed above and
order their return where applicable, and grant such other relief as
is just.

PROOF OF SERVICE: Attach proof of service on the People as required
by Section 1538.5 and local rules.
