FILING NOTES: There is no court filing for this step -- it is a letter
to the agency. If the agency still refuses after this appeal, your next
options are: (1) file a petition for writ of mandate in superior court
under Government Code section 7923.000 (the court can award attorney's
fees to a successful requester), or (2) contact the California Attorney
General's office. Keep copies of your original request, the denial, and
this appeal together -- a court will want the full paper trail.
