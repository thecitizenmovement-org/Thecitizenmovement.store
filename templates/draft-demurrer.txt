DEMURRER TO ACCUSATORY PLEADING
CALIFORNIA PENAL CODE SECTIONS 1002-1004
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

INSTRUCTIONS: A criminal demurrer challenges only the face of the
accusatory pleading (complaint, information, or indictment) and
raises only issues of law. It is NOT a way to dispute facts, argue
that witnesses are lying, or present evidence — those belong at
trial or in other motions. It must be filed before entry of a plea.
If the demurrer is sustained, the court may allow amendment or
dismiss, depending on the ground and the statute. Check the criminal
court's local rules and hearing schedule. Consult a licensed
attorney; a demurrer that attacks facts instead of the pleading will
be overruled.

STATUTE: Penal Code 1002 — the only pleading on the part of the
defendant is either a demurrer or a plea. Penal Code 1004 — the
defendant may demur at any time prior to entry of a plea, when it
appears on the face of the pleading that:
(a) If an indictment, the grand jury had no legal authority to
    inquire into the offense charged, or, if an information or
    complaint, the court has no jurisdiction of the offense charged;
(b) It does not substantially conform to Penal Code 950 and 952
    (and 951 for an indictment or information);
(c) More than one offense is charged, except as provided in
    Section 954;
(d) The facts stated do not constitute a public offense;
(e) It contains matter which, if true, would constitute a legal
    justification or excuse, or other legal bar to prosecution;
(f) The statutory provision alleged is constitutionally invalid.
