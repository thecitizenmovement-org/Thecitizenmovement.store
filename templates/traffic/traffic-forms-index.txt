TRAFFIC FORMS INDEX — CALIFORNIA JUDICIAL COUNCIL, PUBLIC FORMS
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

IMPORTANT: These are MANDATORY Judicial Council forms. Do NOT file a
recreated substitute form in place of the official form — courts
require the official form, and form versions change. This index and
the draft content files beside it tell you which official form to
use, what it is for, who files it, and what content goes in it.
Get the current official form from the California Courts forms
page (courts.ca.gov) or the traffic clerk of the court on your
citation. Check the form revision date before filing.

FORMS IN THE TRAFFIC PROCESS, IN ORDER:
1. TR-200 — Instructions to Defendant (Trial by Written
   Declaration). Read first. Explains Vehicle Code 40902, deadlines,
   bail, and the rights you waive by choosing a written trial
   (including the right to remain silent and not incriminate
   yourself in the written statement).
2. TR-205 — Request for Trial by Written Declaration. THE form the
   defendant files to contest an infraction in writing. Must be
   received by the clerk by the due date on the citation / courtesy
   notice, WITH full bail posted. See the draft statement file
   beside this index for what to write in the Statement of Facts.
3. MC-031 — Attached Declaration. Extra space if your statement
   does not fit in TR-205, and the form witnesses use for their own
   signed statements. Sign and date each page, under penalty of
   perjury.
4. TR-235 — Officer's Declaration. Filed by the citing officer, not
   by the defendant. Listed here so you know what the judge reads
   alongside your declaration.
5. TR-215 — Decision and Notice of Decision. Issued BY the court
   after a written-declaration trial. Outcomes: not guilty, guilty,
   or guilty with reduced fine. Bail is refunded if not guilty.
6. TR-220 — Request for New Trial (Trial de Novo). Filed BY the
   defendant within 20 days of the date the TR-215 decision was
   MAILED, if dissatisfied with a written-declaration decision.
   Gives a brand-new in-person trial, Vehicle Code 40902(d).
7. CR-141-INFO — Information on Appeal Procedures for Infractions.
   Read before appealing an infraction judgment after an in-person
   trial.
8. CR-142 — Notice of Appeal and Record of Oral Proceedings
   (Infraction). The appeal form for infractions, filed with the
   trial court. Check its deadline and record rules on the form
   itself and in CR-141-INFO — do not assume the unlimited-civil
   appeal deadlines apply.

ELIGIBILITY REMINDER (Vehicle Code 40902):
Trial by written declaration is for infractions under the Vehicle
Code or a local traffic ordinance adopted under it. It is NOT
available for misdemeanors, for DUI infractions (Vehicle Code 23152
et seq.), or for citations requiring a mandatory court appearance.
If you have a failure to appear or failure to pay on the case, or
an outstanding warrant, resolve that first — the clerk may not
accept a written-declaration request until the case is in good
standing. If a request is found ineligible, California Rules of
Court 4.210 requires the clerk to extend the deadline by 25
calendar days and notify you by mail.

SOURCES: Vehicle Code 40902; California Rules of Court 4.210;
Judicial Council forms TR-200, TR-205, TR-215, TR-220, TR-235,
MC-031, CR-141-INFO, CR-142 — California Courts (courts.ca.gov).
