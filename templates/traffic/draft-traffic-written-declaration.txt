TRIAL BY WRITTEN DECLARATION — STATEMENT OF FACTS DRAFT
FOR JUDICIAL COUNCIL FORM TR-205 (USE THE OFFICIAL FORM)
VEHICLE CODE SECTION 40902
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

INSTRUCTIONS: File the OFFICIAL TR-205 form, not this draft. This
file is the content to put in the TR-205 Statement of Facts box,
continuing on MC-031 (Attached Declaration) if you need more space.
Submit TR-205 WITH full bail, received by the clerk by the due date
on your citation / courtesy notice. Sign and date each page under
penalty of perjury, using the exact declaration language on TR-200:
"I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the state of
California that this statement is true and correct."
WARNING: By filing a written declaration you waive the right to
remain silent and not incriminate yourself in that statement, and
you waive in-person appearance, testimony, and subpoena of witnesses
for the written round. You keep the right to a trial de novo if you
lose (TR-220, within 20 days of mailing of the decision).

HEADER — FILL IN ON TR-205:
Defendant: [Full name, address, phone]
Citation number: [___]
Court: Superior Court of California, County of [___]
Officer: [Name, agency, if shown on citation]
Violation(s) charged: [Vehicle Code section(s), e.g., 22350]
Date / time / place of alleged violation: [___]
Plea: Not guilty. I request a trial by written declaration under
Vehicle Code 40902.

STATEMENT OF FACTS — FILL IN, IN CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER:
1. On [date] at about [time], I was driving [vehicle] on [road,
   direction, lane] near [intersection / landmark].
2. [What happened, in your own words, step by step: speed, signals,
   signs, traffic, weather, road conditions. State only facts you
   personally observed.]
3. The officer [stopped me / observed me] at [place]. [What the
   officer said and did, as you observed it.]
4. My defense is: [State the defense plainly — e.g., "I was not
   speeding: the posted limit was ___ and I was traveling ___";
   "The stop sign was obscured by ___"; "The equipment was repaired
   on ___ — receipt attached"; "The officer's view was blocked by
   ___". Do not argue law you have not checked; state facts and
   let the facts carry the defense.]

EVIDENCE ATTACHED — LIST EACH ITEM AND DESCRIBE IT HERE:
- [ ] Photographs: [describe each — intersection, signage, vehicle,
  road markings — with date taken]
- [ ] Diagram: [describe — your position, officer's position,
  signs, lanes]
- [ ] Repair receipt / proof of correction: [date, shop, item]
- [ ] Witness declaration(s) (MC-031): [witness name, what they saw]
- [ ] Other: [business record, receipt, or other document — the
  written-declaration rules allow evidence in forms that would not
  always be admissible at an in-person trial, including business
  records and signed letters; list each item so the judge can find
  it]

REQUEST:
I ask the court to find me not guilty of the violation(s) charged,
and to refund the bail posted, for the facts and evidence above.

DECLARATION:
I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the state of
California that this statement is true and correct.
Signed: [Name, date, place] — sign and date EACH page.

AFTER FILING — WHAT HAPPENS:
The clerk notifies the officer, who files a declaration (TR-235).
A judicial officer reviews both declarations and the evidence on
paper and mails a decision (TR-215). If guilty and you are
dissatisfied, file TR-220 within 20 days of the mailing date of
that decision for a trial de novo.

SOURCES: Vehicle Code 40902; California Rules of Court 4.210;
Judicial Council TR-200, TR-205, TR-215, TR-220, TR-235, MC-031.

END — GENERAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE.
