REQUEST FOR NEW TRIAL (TRIAL DE NOVO) — TRAFFIC
FOR JUDICIAL COUNCIL FORM TR-220 (USE THE OFFICIAL FORM)
VEHICLE CODE SECTION 40902(d)
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

INSTRUCTIONS: File the OFFICIAL TR-220 form, not this draft. This
file is a checklist and content guide for that form. A trial de novo
is a completely new in-person trial, as if the written-declaration
trial never happened, before a different judicial officer, where you
can appear, testify, and cross-examine the officer.

DEADLINE — DO NOT MISS IT:
File TR-220 within 20 days of the date the court MAILED the Decision
and Notice of Decision (TR-215) in your trial by written
declaration. Count from the mailing date on the decision, not the
date you received it, and check the form and local rules for how
the court counts the period and whether bail remains posted or must
be re-posted. If you miss this deadline, the written-declaration
decision stands.

HEADER — FILL IN ON TR-220:
Defendant: [Full name, address, phone]
Citation number: [___]
Court: Superior Court of California, County of [___]
Written-declaration decision: [Date mailed on TR-215, outcome]

REQUEST — FILL IN:
I am dissatisfied with the decision in my trial by written
declaration, mailed on [date], and I request a new trial (trial de
novo) under Vehicle Code 40902(d).

IN-PERSON TRIAL CHECKLIST — PREPARE THESE BEFORE THE NEW TRIAL DATE:
- [ ] Arraignment / trial date confirmed with the clerk; bail
  status confirmed (posted, refunded, or to be re-posted).
- [ ] Discovery requested from the prosecution: officer's notes,
  any speed-device calibration logs, photographs, and any other
  evidence the prosecution will use.
- [ ] Witnesses: list each witness, what they will testify to, and
  whether a subpoena is needed (Judicial Council CR-125 for an
  order to attend court / provide documents, where applicable).
- [ ] Evidence organized: photos, diagrams, repair receipts, proof
  of correction, with copies for the court and the prosecution.
- [ ] Traffic school: if eligible and you wish to request it in the
  event of a guilty finding, confirm eligibility and the fee with
  the clerk before trial — do not assume it is automatic.
- [ ] Rights at trial: you may be represented by counsel you retain
  (no court-appointed counsel for infractions), you may testify or
  not, and the prosecution must prove every element of the charged
  infraction.

AFTER THE TRIAL DE NOVO:
If found guilty at the in-person trial, the next step is an
infraction appeal — see CR-141-INFO and form CR-142, filed with the
trial court. Do not use the unlimited-civil appeal template for an
infraction appeal without checking the infraction appeal rules.

SOURCES: Vehicle Code 40902(d); California Rules of Court 4.210;
Judicial Council TR-215, TR-220, CR-141-INFO, CR-142.

END — GENERAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE.
