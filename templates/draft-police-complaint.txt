FILING NOTES: File with the agency's Internal Affairs division or
civilian review board -- every California agency must have a complaint
process. Get the complaint number in writing and keep it. IMPORTANT
DEADLINE: California law generally gives agencies one year from the
incident to complete investigations that could lead to officer
discipline, so file promptly -- delay can kill accountability. You may
also file a complaint with the California Attorney General's office
and, for civil rights violations, with the U.S. Department of Justice
at civilrights.justice.gov. Filing an agency complaint does not stop
any separate lawsuit deadline -- if you were injured, also see the
government tort claim template (6-month deadline).
