MOTION FOR CONTINUANCE OF CRIMINAL HEARING / TRIAL
CALIFORNIA PENAL CODE SECTION 1050
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

INSTRUCTIONS: Criminal continuances are disfavored. Penal Code
1050(e) provides that continuances shall be granted only upon a
showing of good cause, and neither the convenience of the parties
nor a stipulation of the parties is, in itself, good cause. The
court must make a finding on the record whether good cause exists
and, if so, state the facts proved that justify it. A vague "need
more time" will be denied. File and serve written notice, with
affidavits or declarations, at least two court days before the
hearing sought to be continued (Section 1050(b)); if you cannot
meet that notice rule, you must also show good cause for the
failure to comply, or the motion shall not be granted and sanctions
under Section 1050.5 may be imposed. Note the speedy-trial effect:
a continuance you request or consent to may waive time under Penal
Code 1382 — state in the motion whether you are or are not waiving
time, and for how long. Consult a licensed attorney.

STATUTE: Penal Code 1050 — criminal proceedings shall be set for
trial and heard at the earliest possible time. To continue any
hearing, including trial, written notice shall be filed and served
on all parties at least two court days before the hearing, together
with affidavits or declarations. Good cause includes, but is not
limited to, specified circumstances in Section 1050(g), such as a
party's attorney being engaged in another trial, preliminary hearing,
or suppression motion in progress — check the current text for the
full list and any limits (including a 10-court-day limit for that
particular ground).

DECLARATION SHOWING GOOD CAUSE — FILL IN, WITH SPECIFIC FACTS:
1. Good cause is: [Specific facts — e.g., "Defense counsel is in

   received on [date] requires [investigation / expert review] by

2. Why this could not have been avoided: [What was done, when, and

3. Diligence: [Steps taken to be ready, and what remains to be done

4. Effect on other parties and witnesses: [Witness availability,
   custody status, and any prejudice or lack of it.]
5. Time waiver: [Defendant [does / does not] waive time under Penal
   Code 1382 for the period of this continuance, through [date].]

PRAYER:
Moving party prays that the [hearing / trial] set for [date] be
continued to [date], and for such other relief as is just. Proof of
