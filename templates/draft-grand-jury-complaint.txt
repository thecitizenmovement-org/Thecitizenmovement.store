FILING NOTES: Mail or hand-deliver to the grand jury at the address
listed on the county court's website. Some counties have an official
complaint form -- if so, use theirs and attach this as a supplement.
The grand jury is not required to investigate every complaint or to
tell you what it decides. If your matter is urgent or involves an
ongoing crime, also report it to law enforcement directly -- the grand
jury is an oversight body, not emergency responders.
