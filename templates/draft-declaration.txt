DECLARATION IN SUPPORT
(Code of Civil Procedure section 2015.5 -- unsworn declaration
under penalty of perjury, no notary required in California)

INSTRUCTIONS: Use this declaration to attach sworn facts to any motion,
opposition, or other court filing. Write only facts you personally know
-- what you saw, heard, said, or did. Do not argue the law here; save
arguments for the motion itself. Number every paragraph. Attach exhibits
at the end and refer to them by letter or number in the text.

================================================================

FILING NOTES: File this declaration together with the motion it
supports -- it is an attachment, not a standalone filing. File the
original plus 2 copies with the court clerk, and serve a copy on every
other party in the case (see the proof-of-service template for how to
document service). Each declaration gets its own exhibit letters
starting at A; do not mix exhibits between declarations. If a fact is
outside your personal knowledge (something someone told you), either
leave it out or have that person sign their own declaration.

This template provides general legal information only -- not legal
advice. Consult a licensed attorney for your situation.
