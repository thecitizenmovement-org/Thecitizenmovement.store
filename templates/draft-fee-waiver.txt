LEGAL BASIS

Under California Government Code sections 68630 through 68641, the court
must waive initial filing fees and other court costs for a litigant who
receives qualifying public benefits or whose monthly income is at or
below 125 percent of the federal poverty guidelines, or who otherwise
cannot pay court fees without using money needed for basic living
expenses. I meet these criteria as shown in the financial statement
above.

FILING NOTES: File with the clerk of the superior court handling your
case. Bring the original plus 2 copies; the clerk stamps all copies and
returns 2 to you. If you are filing a new case, file this request at the
same time as your first papers. If the court denies the waiver, you will
receive a written order explaining why, and you may submit additional
financial information or request a hearing.
