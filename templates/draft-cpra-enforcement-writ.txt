VERIFIED PETITION FOR WRIT OF MANDATE / COMPLAINT FOR DECLARATORY
AND INJUNCTIVE RELIEF — CALIFORNIA PUBLIC RECORDS ACT ENFORCEMENT
GOVERNMENT CODE SECTION 7923.000 ET SEQ. (FORMERLY 6258)
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

INSTRUCTIONS: This is the enforcement step after a records request
and appeal have failed. It is filed in the superior court of the
county where the records, or some part of them, are situated, as a
verified petition. A court order in a CPRA case is not appealed like
an ordinary judgment — under Government Code 7923.500 it is
reviewable by petition for extraordinary writ, within the time the
statute sets (20 days after service of notice of entry, with a
limited additional period). Check the superior court's local rules,
the civil case cover sheet, and the current CPRA text before filing.
The CPRA was recodified effective January 1, 2023, from Government
Code 6250 et seq. to 7920 et seq. — cite the current numbers below,
and note the former numbers in parentheses where helpful.

STATUTE: Government Code 7923.000 (former 6258) — any person may
institute a proceeding for injunctive or declarative relief, or for
a writ of mandate, in any court of competent jurisdiction, to
enforce that person's right to inspect or receive a copy of any
public record or class of public records. Government Code 7923.100
(former 6259) — on a verified petition showing records are being
improperly withheld, the court shall order the officer or person
charged with withholding to disclose the records or show cause why
not. A prevailing plaintiff in CPRA litigation is entitled to costs
and reasonable attorney fees under the CPRA enforcement chapter —
check the current fee section before pleading it by number.

7923.000): The records described above are public records, no
exemption cited justifies withholding them in full, and Respondent
has a mandatory duty to disclose them or show cause.
Second Cause (Declaratory Relief): An actual controversy exists over
whether the records are disclosable, and a declaration is needed to
resolve it.
