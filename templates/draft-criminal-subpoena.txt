CRIMINAL SUBPOENA — REQUEST AND SUBPOENA DUCES TECUM GUIDE
CALIFORNIA PENAL CODE SECTIONS 1326-1327
THE CITIZEN MOVEMENT — GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION ONLY, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

INSTRUCTIONS: This is a guide and draft request, not a substitute
for the subpoena form itself. In a criminal case, a subpoena may be
signed and issued by, among others, a magistrate or clerk, the
district attorney or investigator, the public defender or
investigator, the clerk of the court (who, on application of the
defendant and without charge, shall issue as many blank subpoenas
for witnesses in the state as the defendant may require), or the
attorney of record for the defendant (Penal Code 1326(a)). Two
types: subpoena ad testificandum (compels testimony) and subpoena
duces tecum (compels production of records / things). No good-cause
affidavit is required to ISSUE a criminal subpoena duces tecum, but
to DEFEND it against a motion to quash you must then show good
cause — more than a mere desire for all information — and the court
balances factors including relevance, specificity, and burden (see
Facebook, Inc. v. Superior Court (2020) for the third-party
subpoena factors). Serve properly, allow time for compliance or
objection, and be ready for an in-camera review dispute over
privileged or sensitive records. Consult a licensed attorney before
subpoenaing medical, communications, or other sensitive records.

REQUEST TO CLERK FOR BLANK SUBPOENAS — FILL IN:
To the Clerk, Superior Court of California, County of [___]:
Defendant [name], Case No. [___], requests [number] blank
subpoena(s) for witnesses in the state, under Penal Code 1326(a)(3),
for trial / hearing on [date, department].
Signed: [Defendant / attorney of record, date].

SUBPOENA DUCES TECUM — FILL IN (USE THE COURT'S SUBPOENA FORM):
THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA, TO: [Person / entity /
custodian of records, name and address].
YOU ARE COMMANDED to appear in Superior Court of California,
County of [___], Department [___], on [date, time], and to bring
with you the following records / things, described with specificity:
[Describe each item separately — e.g., "1. [Record type, date
range, subject]." Do not demand "all records" without limits.]
These records are sought because: [Relevance — how each item could
lead to admissible evidence or support a defense, in plain terms.
This section is your good-cause showing if the subpoena is
challenged, so be specific.]
Service: Serve a copy on the witness / custodian and, as required,
on the opposing party, and file proof of service. Note the
appearance date, the place for production, and any objection
deadline the court or local rules set.

IF THE SUBPOENA IS CHALLENGED (MOTION TO QUASH) — BE READY TO SHOW:
1. Relevance and materiality to a defense or issue in the case.
2. Specificity — the request is narrowly described, not a fishing
   expedition.
3. That the records are not available from a less burdensome source.
4. That privilege, privacy, or a statute (including the Stored
   Communications Act for electronic communications) does not bar
   production, or that any protected portion can be redacted or
   reviewed in camera.

WARNING: Failure to obey a properly served subpoena can result in
contempt proceedings. Do not use a subpoena to harass a witness or
to obtain records you know are privileged without a court order.
